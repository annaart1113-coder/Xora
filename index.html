FrequentlyAsked questions

Everything you need to know

  • Xora is a self-custody wallet: your private keys are encrypted and stored only on your device, never on our servers. Access is protected with a PIN and biometric login, and every transaction must be confirmed by you.

  • Your funds live on the blockchain, not in the phone. Install Xora on any device, enter your 12-word recovery phrase and your wallet is restored. Keep the phrase offline and never share it with anyone.

  • Yes. Xora supports 3,500+ coins and tokens across 50+ networks, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tron and USDT, all in one balance.

  • Yes. Instant Swap lets you trade one token for another in a few taps. You see the rate and all fees before you confirm, and the swap goes straight to your wallet.

  • Downloading and holding crypto in Xora is free. Sending crypto costs only the network fee, and swaps or purchases include a small service fee that is always shown up front.

  • Blockchain transfers can’t be reversed once confirmed. That’s why Xora checks the address format, warns you about suspicious addresses and asks you to review every detail before sending.

  • No verification is needed to store, send, receive or swap crypto. Buying crypto with a bank card through our partners may require a quick identity check, as their regulations demand.
